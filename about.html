Professional Background

Bridging 15+ years of manual testing rigor with cutting-edge AI test engineering and data analytics.

My Approach to Quality Engineering

Over the past 15 years, I've tested platforms where defects had real consequences, such as an airline booking failing, an insurance policy miscalculating, or a healthcare copay getting delayed. That experience taught me early on that great testing isn't about clicking every button or writing hundreds of low-value checks; it's about understanding the business, anticipating where systems break, and fiercely protecting the workflows that matter most to users.

Why I Embrace AI in Testing

When Generative AI emerged, I didn't see it as a replacement for human judgment. I saw it as the best way to eliminate QA's biggest bottleneck: repetitive test drafting. Because I know how test cases should be structured from years of hands-on testing, I can prompt and chain LLMs to generate exhaustive negative and boundary tests in seconds. It frees testers from tedious boilerplate so we can focus our energy on exploratory testing, architecture, and finding the subtle bugs that automated scripts miss.

Connecting Testing to Data Analytics

Through my graduate studies in Business Analytics at Mapúa University, I connect testing directly to data. Every bug report, test run, and regression cycle generates valuable insights. By applying analytics to defect trends and root causes, I help engineering teams move from simply catching bugs at the end of a sprint to actively preventing them before release day.
